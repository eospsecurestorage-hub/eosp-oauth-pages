Privacy Policy — EOSP rclone

Last updated: 5 November 2026

EOSP rclone is a private Google Drive storage integration used by the owner of the European Offline Survival Platform (EOSP) project for development-related storage and backups.

Google Drive access

The integration requests the Google Drive drive.file OAuth scope, allowing it to create and manage files it creates or that are explicitly made available to the application. It does not request unrestricted access to all Google Drive files.

Information processed

The integration processes OAuth credentials and the Google Drive files authorized for its use. Files may be uploaded, downloaded, checked for integrity, or deleted when expressly authorized by the owner.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored in an encrypted rclone configuration. Access to files and credentials is limited operationally, although encryption of the configuration does not prevent access by privileged processes while the integration is running.

Sharing and retention

The integration is not offered to public users. It does not intentionally sell or share Google user data with advertisers. Files remain in the owner's Google Drive account until removed by the owner or an authorized operation. OAuth access can be revoked through Google Account settings.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact eosp.secure.storage@gmail.com.
